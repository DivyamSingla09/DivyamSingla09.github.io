Electric Turbine
Jet Engine

A working jet engine demonstrator built from scratch as a team project: a BLDC motor spins a 3D-printed centrifugal compressor that feeds air into a combustion chamber, where gas fuel is ignited by a spark plug and exits through the exhaust.

Overview

The idea

A real gas-turbine engine uses its own turbine to drive the compressor. To make a safe, buildable demonstrator, we replaced that loop with an electric drive: a high-speed BLDC motor spins the compressor directly, so the airflow can be controlled by the throttle from the ESC.

Air enters through the closed inlet and the centrifugal impeller pressurises it. In the combustion chamber, gas fuel comes in through the fuel inlet and is lit by a spark plug fired from a high-voltage ignition module. The hot gases then leave through the outer body and exhaust nozzle. The whole engine sits on a wooden test bench with the battery and speed controller alongside.

I worked on the CAD of the 3D-printed parts and on the fabrication and assembly of the engine.

Electronics & ignition

Drive system

Battery pack → ESC → BLDC motor, with a servo tester acting as the throttle knob. Shown with the 3D-printed impeller in its housing.

Ignition system

A separate battery powers a high-voltage arc module that fires a spark plug inside the combustion chamber to light the fuel.

Process

How it was built

  1. Concept & sizingChose an electrically driven compressor and sized the impeller and housings around the BLDC motor.
  2. CAD modellingModelled the impeller, motor holder, inlet ring, inlet lid and motor cap with bolt patterns for assembly.
  3. 3D printingPrinted all five parts in PLA and fitted the impeller to the motor shaft.
  4. Metal fabricationFabricated the mild-steel combustion chamber and body and joined them with MIG welding.
  5. Ignition systemFitted a spark plug in the combustion chamber, powered by a high-voltage arc module and a separate battery pack.
  6. Assembly & electronicsMounted everything on a wooden test bench and wired the motor, ESC, servo tester and battery.
  7. TestingSpun up the compressor from the servo tester, fed gas through the fuel inlet and fired the ignition on the bench.
